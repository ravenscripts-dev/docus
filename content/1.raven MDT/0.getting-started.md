# Raven MDT

Raven MDT is a powerful, versatile Mobile Data Terminal solution designed specifically for FiveM servers. Built to support **ESX** and **QBox** frameworks, Raven MDT integrates seamlessly into your server's roleplay ecosystem, providing law enforcement and emergency services with advanced data access, vehicle management, and more—all from a mobile, in-game interface.

---

## Key Features

- **Framework Support**  
  Fully compatible with both ESX and QBox frameworks, ensuring smooth integration with your server’s economy and structure.

- **Mobile Data Access**  
  Access critical information such as vehicle data, criminal records, and dispatch communications right from the MDT interface, enabling faster and more efficient decision-making in the field.

- **Fines and Licensing**  
  Built-in fines system allows officers to issue tickets and fines directly from the MDT, with real-time updates to the player's fines and license status. The license export feature ensures vehicle and driver information is up-to-date.

- **Real-Time Data Sync**  
  Stay connected with real-time data updates to ensure that your officers always have the latest information, whether they’re patrolling or responding to emergencies.

- **Customizable Interface**  
  The interface is designed to be highly customizable, allowing you to tailor the look and feel of the MDT to suit your server's unique needs and style.

- **Robust and Reliable**  
  The script is designed with performance in mind, ensuring low server resource usage while delivering a seamless experience for your players.

- **Easy Installation and Configuration**  
  Raven MDT is easy to set up with simple configuration options, allowing you to get it up and running with minimal effort.

- **Advanced Permissions and Roles**  
  Fully integrated with your server's permission system to ensure that only authorized personnel can access certain features, such as issuing fines or viewing sensitive data.

- **Licensing Support**  
  Includes license management features, allowing officers to check the validity of driver’s licenses and vehicle registrations directly through the MDT.

---

## Transform Your Server

Transform your FiveM server’s law enforcement capabilities with **Raven MDT**, offering advanced, real-time data access, fine management, and licensing checks—all built into a robust, intuitive mobile interface. With support for both **ESX** and **QBox**, Raven MDT is the ultimate MDT solution for any roleplay server.
